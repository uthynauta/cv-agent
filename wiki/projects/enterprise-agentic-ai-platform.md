# Enterprise Agentic AI Platform
- Internal enterprise platform for deploying, consuming, evaluating, and observing AI agents at [[entities/teradata]].
- Engineered LLM workflows with LangGraph, structured tool execution, reusable agent skills, MCP integrations, and [[concepts/retrieval-augmented-generation|RAG]].
- Designed observability and distributed tracing with OpenTelemetry, including token usage, latency, cost, errors, model metadata, tool calls, execution paths, and outcomes.
- Managed Grafana, Prometheus, Loki, and tracing pipelines for debugging, performance analysis, and FinOps.
- Built telemetry and evaluation pipelines with PostgreSQL/pgvector and DeepEval.
- Developed Docker Compose and Kubernetes environments for multi-service development, testing, and production readiness.
