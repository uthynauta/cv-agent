# CentroGeo Multimodal Research

## Role and period
- Postdoctoral Researcher at CentroGeo, Jul 2021–Jul 2023.

## Research
- Developed NLP and multimodal deep-learning models for generative tasks, especially automatic image and video captioning.
- Architected and trained recurrent neural network, LSTM, attention, and Transformer models for vision-language problems.
- Designed evaluation workflows to assess whether caption-generation metrics reflected semantic and perceptual quality.
- Used PyTorch, Transformers, and Hugging Face for model development, experimentation, training, and inference.
- Managed datasets and computational workflows for reproducible training, inference, and comparison.
- Evaluated DALL-E and Stable Diffusion for research-oriented applications.
- Co-authored publications on video captioning, multimodal-model evaluation, and computer vision.
