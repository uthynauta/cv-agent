# Multimodal Captioning Research
- Role: Postdoctoral Researcher at CentroGeo, July 2021 – July 2023.
- Researched NLP and multimodal deep-learning models for generative tasks, especially automatic image and video captioning.
- Developed recurrent neural network, LSTM, attention, and Transformer models for vision-language problems.
- Designed experiments to assess whether common caption-generation metrics reflected semantic and perceptual quality.
- Used PyTorch, Transformers, and Hugging Face for model development, training, experimentation, and inference.
- Evaluated DALL-E and Stable Diffusion for research-oriented applications.
- Co-authored peer-reviewed publications on video captioning, multimodal-model evaluation, and computer vision.
