# UAV and Computer Vision Research
- Othón González completed a PhD in Computer Vision at CICATA Querétaro, IPN, in January 2020.
- Dissertation: precise remote measurement of thermal radiance dynamics using UAVs.
- Completed an MSc in Aerodynamic Optimization at CICATA Querétaro, IPN, with research on a data-driven morphable wing for tracking and mapping using small UAVs.
- At [[entities/centrogeo]], researched NLP models for generative tasks, including automated image captioning.
- Worked with DALL-E and Stable Diffusion and trained RNN, LSTM, and Transformer models for computer vision and language research.
- Publications include work on infrared thermal camera calibration, UAV thermal radiation, video captioning, and image-captioning metric evaluation.
