# Othón González — Education and Research
- PhD research focused on computer vision and remote sensing; thesis: “Precise Remote Measurement of Thermal Radiance Dynamics Using Unmanned Aerial Vehicles.”
- MSc research focused on aerodynamic optimization and morphing systems; thesis: “Data-Driven Morphable Wing for Tracking and Mapping Using Small Unmanned Aerial Vehicles.”
- As a CentroGeo postdoctoral researcher from July 2021 to July 2023, researched NLP and multimodal deep-learning models for image and video captioning.
- Developed recurrent neural network, LSTM, attention, and Transformer models for vision-language research problems.
- Evaluated caption-generation systems and whether common metrics reflected semantic and perceptual output quality.
- Co-authored publications on video captioning, multimodal-model evaluation, computer vision, and thermal imaging.
